What LuPass is

LuPass is a study tool for the Chinese driver's license written test (科目一 and 科目四) for foreigners. It contains a question bank translated into English, practice modes, mock exams, and progress tracking.

LuPass is not affiliated with the Public Security Bureau, the Ministry of Public Security, any DMV (车管所), or any official Chinese government body. Passing the practice exams in this app does not guarantee passing the real test. Use this app to study; the real test is at the DMV.
